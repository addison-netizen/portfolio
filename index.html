From inquiry to follow-up

Concept / Automation

A connected workflow that could keep new inquiries organized and make the next step easier to remember.

Explore the workflow

The idea: A contact form starts the workflow. It checks the required details, looks for an existing contact, and adds or updates a record in a spreadsheet or CRM.

The next step: Prepare an acknowledgment email for review and create a follow-up task. Incomplete submissions go to a review queue instead of getting lost.

Built around your tools: The form, email account, tracker, and follow-up timing would be chosen for your business. This is an illustrative workflow, not a running integration.

From intake to organized drafts

Concept / Automation

A document workflow that could cut repeated typing and keep each draft ready for a person to review.

Explore the workflow

The idea: Turn an intake form into a draft proposal, project brief, or welcome packet using your own template.

The next step: Check for missing information, create a consistently named folder, save the draft, and notify the reviewer. Sending the final document stays a separate approval step.

Built around your tools: The form, document templates, storage, and review process would be tailored to your business. This is an illustrative workflow, not a running integration.
